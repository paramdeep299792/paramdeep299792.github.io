# Projects & Research

## On-going Research Projects

**XAI-FL** *(2026)*  
* Trying to integrate XAI (Explainable Artificial Intelligence) on models trained using [Federated Learning (FL)](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1602.05629), with a focus on privacy-preserving techniques like Differential Privacy.
* **Guides:** [Dr. Parteek Kumar Bhatia](https://school.eecs.wsu.edu/directory/wsu-profile/parteek.kumar/) (WSU, USA) and [Dr. Sachin Kansal](https://csed.thapar.edu/facultydetails/MTQyNA==) (TIET, India).
* **Tech Stack:** PyTorch, InterpretML, NVFlare.

**BioFL** *(2026)*  
* Applying Federated Learning (FL) to unexplored biomedical datasets.
* **Guides:** Dr. Parteek Kumar Bhatia (WSU, USA) and [Dr. Kashish Goyal](https://csed.thapar.edu/facultydetails/MTcxNg==) (TIET, India).
* **Tech Stack:** PyTorch, InterpretML.

**Deepfake Detection** *(2026)*  
* Building a model to detect deepfakes in datasets featuring audio-video modality.
* **Guide:** [Dr. Shefali Arora Chouhan](https://departments.nitj.ac.in/dept/cse/Faculty/6430447138bff038a7808ce8) (NIT Jalandhar, India).
* **Tech Stack:** PyTorch.
